Someone put a bounty on your head. Now everyone in Port Halden, Ashford and Saltmarsh Docks wants it.

Survive 30 days. Keep your head down and you can blend into the crowd: walk, keep your weapon away and don't hang about. Running, waving guns and spending cash make people wonder. Police helicopters and drones only watch, and they lose you between buildings.

Hide out in your nan's old flat (the white house on the map). Get in unseen and you can lie low, raid the stash and nap.

Superpower mode: you are the city's greatest hero, and everyone is terrified of you. Click for laser eyes, Space twice to fly, C in the air to slam down, E to grab someone (click to throw), F to swat people and cars. More or less unkillable.
